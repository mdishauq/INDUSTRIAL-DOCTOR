====================================

--------------------------

2. KEY FINDINGS IN AI TRAINING
-------------------------------
- Battery-Acoustic Relationship: The data captures the transition of the motor as voltage drops from 6V toward 4.8V. This defines the "Low Battery" healthy profile.
- Frequency Resilience: Despite the voltage drop, the motor maintained a dominant resonance mode at 720.0 Hz. The shift from the 8V baseline (680Hz) helps create a "Frequency Corridor."
- Low-Instability Signature: Even during power degradation, RMS_Stab remained low (17.37), confirming that internal battery discharge is a "Stable" event unlike "Unstable" external noise.

3. STRATEGY FOR ADVANCED VOTING SYSTEM
--------------------------------------
- The "Low Power" Vote: The system will now recognize any PeakHz between 500Hz and 800Hz as "Healthy" when RMS is below 350.
- Voltage-Aware Consensus: By mapping 6V and 8V, the voting system can use a "Sliding Window" for frequency. If voltage is low, it expects a different peak frequency.
- Fault Prevention: This data provides the baseline to prevent the BMS from misidentifying a slowing motor (due to low battery) as a mechanical bearing failure.
