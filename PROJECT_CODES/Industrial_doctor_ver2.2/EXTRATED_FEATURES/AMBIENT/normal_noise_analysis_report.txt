--------------------------------------

the voting thresholds:
- Veto Rule 1: If RMS_Stab > 15.0, the 'Silent' and 'Normal' votes are invalidated.
- Veto Rule 2: If ZCR > 0.05, it triggers a 'Non-Mechanical Noise' flag.
- Consensus: The system requires agreement across Stability, Frequency, and
