The Bodge Wiring Plan
Most 5-way tactile switch modules have a common ground (COM or GND) and output pins that connect to that ground when pressed.

GND: Connect to any ground pad on your PCB.

3.3V: (Leave disconnected. Most simple tactile switch modules don't actually need VCC unless they have built-in pull-up resistors or an LED. If it strictly requires VCC, wire it to your 3.3V header).

MID (Select): Solder a bodge wire to GPIO 15

UP: Solder a bodge wire to GPIO 4

DOWN: Solder a bodge wire to GPIO 5

LEFT: Solder a bodge wire to GPIO 6

RIGHT: Solder a bodge wire to GPIO 7
